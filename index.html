Questions

Do I get the source code?

Yes. You receive the .pine or .mq5 source, the compiled file where it applies, and a short guide.

Will the indicator repaint?

No. Signals are based on closed bars only. If your idea needs data that is confirmed later, such as swing points, I tell you before I start and show it clearly on the chart.

How long does it take?

A simple indicator usually takes 1–3 days, an EA or strategy 3–7 days. You get the exact time with the quote.

Is my strategy kept private?

Yes. Your rules and your code are never shared or reused.

Can you guarantee profits?

No, and nobody honest can. I make sure the code does exactly what your rules say, and I show you how those rules behaved on past data.
